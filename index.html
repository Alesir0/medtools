HEART Score

History, ECG, Age, Risk Factors, Troponin

1. History

Slightly suspicious — 0
Moderately suspicious — 1
Highly suspicious — 2

2. ECG

Normal — 0
Non-specific repolarization disturbance — 1
Significant ST deviation — 2

3. Age

< 45 years — 0
45–64 years — 1
≥ 65 years — 2

4. Risk Factors

No risk factors — 0
1–2 risk factors — 1
≥3 risk factors / known atherosclerotic disease — 2

5. Troponin

≤ normal limit — 0
1–3 × normal limit — 1
>3 × normal limit — 2
